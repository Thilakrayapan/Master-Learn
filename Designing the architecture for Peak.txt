Designing the architecture for **PeakPulse** (your solo-journey productivity app) requires a system that is both technically robust for data tracking and flexible enough to handle "funny" rewards and punishments.

Since you are already exploring **Python, SQL, and Web Development**, this architecture is designed to leverage those skills while keeping the "Empathy-Driven" core we discussed previously.

---

## **1. The Full Architecture: "The Peak Engine"**

The system follows a **Three-Tier Architecture** with a focus on real-time feedback and behavioral logic.

### **A. Frontend (The "Interface of Action")**
* **Framework:** **Next.js 14** or **Flutter**. This allows for a smooth, app-like experience with fast transitions.
* **The Gradient Grid (D3.js):** Instead of a standard calendar, you'll use a custom SVG-based heatmap. It visualizes your consistency across subjects using color intensity.
* **The Vibe-Check Module:** A simple UI component that captures your mood before and after a study session using emojis or text.

### **B. Backend (The "Logic & Punishment Center")**
* **API Layer:** **FastAPI (Python)**. It's high-performance and perfect for handling your logic.
* **The Scheduler (Celery + Redis):** This is the "brain" that tracks your one-month exam timeline. If a task isn't marked "Done" by its deadline, it triggers the **Punishment Logic**.
* **Sentiment Microservice:** A small Python script using a library like `TextBlob` or `VADER` to analyze your journal entries and adjust the "Motivational Tone" of the app.

### **C. Data Layer (The "Knowledge Vault")**
* **PostgreSQL:** Your primary relational database. It stores:
    * **Subjects & Chapters:** Hierarchical data for your exams.
    * **The Audit Log:** A record of every task completed (or missed).
* **Redis:** Stores "Ephemeral State"—like your current study timer or your active reward streak.

---

## **2. System Workflow Diagram**


1.  **Input:** You set an exam date (e.g., May 15th).
2.  **Decomposition:** The backend breaks subjects into daily chapter "Quests."
3.  **Monitoring:** As you work, the **Timer** sends heartbeats to the server.
4.  **Evaluation:**
    * **Success:** Triggers the "Reward Engine" (New badge, funny meme).
    * **Failure:** Triggers the "Punishment Engine" (App-icon change, "Nag" notifications).

## **3. Future Update Roadmap (The "Peak" Vision)**

Once the core version is stable, here is how we scale it to make it even more "Peak Level":

### **Phase 2: Intelligent Adaptivity (The "AI Tutor" Era)**
* **Smart Rescheduling:** If you consistently struggle with "Theory of Computation" but breeze through "SQL," the AI will automatically shift more time to the harder subject in your timeline.
* **Voice Motivation:** Integration with Text-to-Speech so your "Drill Sergeant" or "Zen Master" can actually speak to you during study breaks.

### **Phase 3: Integration & Ecosystem**
* **IDE Integration:** A VS Code extension that automatically updates your "Skill Pillar" progress when you write Python code.
* **External API Sync:** Syncing with your **Duolingo** streak and **LeetCode** profile so you don't have to manual-entry your skill development.

### **Phase 4: Physical "Punishment" & Hardware**
* **IoT Smart Bulb Integration:** If you miss a task, your room's smart bulb turns red. If you’re in "Focus Mode," it turns a deep, productive purple.
* **Wearable Sync:** Tracking sleep and heart rate to tell you: *"Thilak, your stress is high. Skip the punishment this time and take a 20-minute nap. That is your new task."*
